# Rejected / reversed

Closed doors. Do not re-propose these unless the author explicitly reopens them and updates `CONTEXT.md`.

**Instrument-level guitar front end**
No Terrarium-style guitar booster, high-impedance buffer, or instrument-level in/out. Those stages are why guitar pedals clip or feel wrong on the Play and the S-1. The Seed codec already wants line level. Reopen only when guitar becomes a source, or user mentions he wants to tackle-instrument level signal.
